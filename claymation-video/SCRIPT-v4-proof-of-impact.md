**Length:** ~55s · **Style:** Guldies-inspired plasticine stop-motion, seamless morph transitions, 24fps
**Hero:** Barakatou — Black woman in human claymation form (warm brown plasticine, blazer, gold earrings)

**Source:** BData_Solutions_Website_Content.docx (real offers & pricing)

## Scene 1 — "Served, but not safer?" (0:00–0:12)
Clay caseworker at a shelter desk, vulnerable clay women waiting nearby. Her "500 served" report crumbles into a flood of tangled spreadsheets.
VO: "Your shelter can say how many people you served. But can you show funders whether they got safer… housed… stable? That gap costs you credibility, grant season time, and clarity."

## Scene 2 — "Barakatou arrives" (0:12–0:22)
The tangle morphs upward into Barakatou, who pulls the caseworker out of the paper flood; a clay laptop blooms open.
VO: "Meet BData Solutions — data and analytics built for this sector, by an analyst with twenty years of experience and doctoral research on domestic violence, homelessness, and precarious status. She doesn't just know the spreadsheet. She knows the work."

## Scene 3 — "The transformation" (0:22–0:34)
The flood morphs into a glowing impact dashboard — bars rising, labels "safer / housed / stable". The women gather, steady and bright.
VO: "Impact dashboards in the tools you already use — Google Sheets, Looker Studio, or Power BI — showing the outcomes funders actually want to see."
