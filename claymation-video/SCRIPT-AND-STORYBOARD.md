# BData Solutions — Claymation Promo Video
**Length:** ~45 seconds · **Style:** Stop-motion claymation (Aardman-inspired) · **Website:** www.barakabelle.ca

## Concept
"From Messy Data to Smooth Solutions" — a friendly clay character named **B** (a round, teal blob with big expressive eyes) wrestles a tangled mountain of clay "data spaghetti" and sculpts it into clean, organized shapes, showing how BData Solutions tames business data with Dynamics 365 & Power Platform.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:08)
A cluttered clay office. Papers, sticky notes, and tangled clay wires everywhere. A stressed clay business owner sighs as a wobbly tower of clay spreadsheets topples over.
**VO:** "Drowning in spreadsheets? Customer info scattered everywhere?"

### Scene 2 — Enter B (0:08–0:16)
**B**, a cheerful teal clay blob wearing tiny glasses, rolls in and gives a thumbs up. B stamps a clay logo: "BData Solutions."
**VO:** "Meet BData Solutions — your Dynamics 365 and Power Platform experts."

### Scene 3 — The Transformation (0:16–0:30)
B kneads the tangled clay mess like dough. In satisfying stop-motion, it reshapes into neat colorful blocks: a CRM dashboard, automated flows (little clay conveyor belts), and happy customer figurines lining up in order.
**VO:** "We turn messy data into smart CRM systems, automated workflows, and insights you can actually use."

### Scene 4 — The Happy Ending (0:30–0:40)
The business owner, now relaxed, sips clay coffee while dashboards glow on a clay monitor. B high-fives them; confetti made of tiny clay dots rains down.
**VO:** "Less chaos. More growth. Built around your business."

### Scene 5 — Call to Action (0:40–0:45)
Clean backdrop. Clay letters press into frame one by one: **BData Solutions** — then **www.barakabelle.ca** stamps in underneath with a squash-and-wobble.
**VO:** "BData Solutions. Visit www.barakabelle.ca today."

## Visual style notes
- Palette: teal & deep blue (tech/trust) with warm orange accents; visible fingerprints in the clay for authentic handmade feel
- 12 fps stop-motion cadence, slight jitter, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field
- Sound: playful ukulele/marimba track, squishy clay foley, gentle "boing" on text stamps
