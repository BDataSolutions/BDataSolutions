# BData Solutions — "Rescued by Barakatou" Video Campaign

**Audience:** Nonprofits, government agencies, advocacy groups (especially those serving vulnerable women)
**Hero:** Barakatou — a confident Black woman in claymation (orange headwrap, teal blazer, gold earrings) who rescues organizations drowning in messy data
**Contact:** hello@barakabelle.ca · hellobdatasolutions@gmail.com · www.barakabelle.ca

## Campaign message
Mission-driven organizations lose time, funding, and trust to scattered spreadsheets and broken systems — and the communities they serve pay the price. BData Solutions (Barakatou) pulls their data out of the mess with Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, so they can get back to the people who need them.

**Tagline:** *Data with heart, for missions that matter.*
