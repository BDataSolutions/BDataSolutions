# AI Video Generation Prompts — BData Solutions Claymation

Copy-paste these into an AI video generator (Google Veo 3 in Gemini, Runway Gen-4, Kling, or Pika).
Generate each scene as its own 8-second clip, then stitch them in CapCut, Canva Video Editor, or Clipchamp and add the voiceover.

**Global style suffix — append to every prompt:**
> Stop-motion claymation style, handmade plasticine with visible fingerprints, 12fps stop-motion cadence, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, teal and deep blue palette with warm orange accents, charming Aardman-style animation, 16:9.

## Scene 1 — The Problem
> A stressed clay business-owner character with big expressive eyes sits at a cluttered clay office desk. A wobbly tower of tiny clay spreadsheets and papers teeters and topples over in slow stop-motion. Tangled clay wires labeled with tiny icons spill across the desk. The character sighs, shoulders slumping.

## Scene 2 — Enter B
> A cheerful round teal clay blob character wearing tiny round glasses rolls into a clay office, bounces to a stop, and gives an enthusiastic thumbs up. It stamps a small clay sign reading "BData Solutions" onto the desk with a satisfying squash.

## Scene 3 — The Transformation
> A teal clay blob character kneads a giant tangled ball of multicolored clay wires like bread dough. In satisfying stop-motion morphing, the mess reshapes into neat glowing blocks: a miniature clay CRM dashboard with charts, tiny clay conveyor belts moving documents automatically, and a row of smiling clay customer figurines lining up in perfect order.

## Scene 4 — The Happy Ending
> A relaxed clay business-owner character leans back sipping from a tiny clay coffee mug while colorful clay dashboards glow on a clay computer monitor. The teal blob character high-fives the owner. Tiny clay confetti dots rain down joyfully.

## Scene 5 — Call to Action
> On a clean pastel backdrop, chunky clay letters press into frame one by one spelling "BData Solutions", each landing with a squash-and-wobble. Below it, smaller clay letters stamp in reading "www.barakabelle.ca". Gentle camera push-in.

## Assembly checklist
1. Generate the 5 clips (≈8s each; trim to storyboard timings).
2. Record or AI-generate the voiceover (ElevenLabs / Canva AI voice) from `SCRIPT-AND-STORYBOARD.md`.
3. Stitch clips + VO + a playful ukulele/marimba track; add squishy clay foley.
4. Export 1080p MP4 (16:9 for the website/YouTube; optionally re-crop 9:16 for Reels/Shorts).
